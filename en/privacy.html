Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what data the Guide du Détour iPhone app (including its CarPlay display) and the guide-du-detour.fr website process, why, and what your rights are, under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, EU 2016/679) and French data-protection law. The French version (Politique de confidentialité) is the reference text.

1. Controller

Détours Studio, represented by Andrey Miras. Contact: andrey@guide-du-detour.fr.

2. In short

  • No ads, no ad trackers, no audience analytics, no data sold.
  • Your live location is used for guidance. We don’t store it and we don’t track your movements.
  • Without an account (guest mode), we keep no personal data about you on our servers.
  • With an account, we keep what is needed to provide the service (trips, favourites, places visited, reviews). You can delete your account from within the app.

4. The guide-du-detour.fr website

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Fonts are served from our own server. Our host keeps technical logs (IP address, date, page requested) for security and operation, for a short period.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Some providers (Google, Mapbox, Apple, Vercel) are based in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

7. What we don’t do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No profiling for commercial purposes.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No background location tracking outside a navigation you started.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and you can withdraw consent at any time (for example by turning off location access in iOS Settings). You can delete your account and its data directly from your profile in the app, or by writing to andrey@guide-du-detour.fr. We reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can complain to the French data-protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or to your local authority.

9. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Server-side security rules restrict access so that each user can only read their own private data.

10. Children

The service is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data about children under 16.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the last update is shown at the top; for significant changes we will let you know in the app.
