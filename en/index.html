    FAQ

    Before you set off

    Is the app free?

    Yes, the essentials are free and ad-free. An optional paid tier will offer more for people who travel a lot.

    Which areas are covered?

    All of mainland France and Corsica.

    Which languages?

    French, English, German and Russian. The interface, voice and instructions follow your iPhone’s language.

    Do I need an account?

    No, guest mode lets you plan a route and navigate. A free account saves your favourite places and visits.

    How are detours chosen?

    The algorithm looks at your route, your detour corridor, the time of day, the weather and opening hours. When in doubt, it suggests nothing rather than a poor stop.

    Does it work with CarPlay?

    Yes. The route, places to see and detour suggestions appear on your car’s screen.

    What about without signal?

    Navigation needs a connection for now. An offline mode is planned.
